个人简介About

关于我About Me

金亮现工作于国防科技大学电子科学学院，研究兴趣包括多输入多输出（MIMO）通信、智能编码与智能传输。相关研究涵盖全息 MIMO、时空信道编码、可重构智能表面及面向下一代无线网络的传输设计。

Liang Jin is with the College of Electronic Science, National University of Defense Technology. His research interests include multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) communications, intelligent coding, and intelligent transmission, with recent work spanning holographic MIMO, spatiotemporal channel coding, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, and transmission design for next-generation wireless networks.
